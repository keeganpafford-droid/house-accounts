Getting your data in

How do I get my customer and order history into House Accounts?

These guides show you how to export your existing customer and order data from the system you already use, and upload it into House Accounts in just a few minutes.

Start here

What to export

Upload a customer or account export -- the businesses you already sell to, not a list of companies you have never worked with. Order history significantly improves House Accounts' reorder and follow-up recommendations, but it is not required to get started.

House Accounts is for existing customers

House Accounts is built around your existing customers and house accounts. In the current beta, please do not upload cold prospect lists or companies you have no relationship with -- that is not what this import is for.

Minimum useful columns

Customer / Company Name

Company name alone is enough for House Accounts to begin monitoring an account for public business signals.

Ideal columns

Customer / Company Name Project or Order Name Order Date Revenue Contact Name Contact Email Margin

Order date, revenue, and project/order name are what unlock repeat-buying patterns, reorder windows, and follow-up timing. Contact fields let House Accounts address the right person directly.

Upload troubleshooting

Common upload issues

"Could not identify a company/account column"

House Accounts needs a company/customer name column. Accepted header names include client_name, company_name, company, account_name, account, customer_name, customer, and client -- rename your column header to one of these, or use the sample template above as a starting point.

The file uploaded, but reorder/follow-up recommendations look limited

This is expected when order date and revenue were not included or not recognized -- House Accounts will still monitor these accounts for public business triggers. Add order history when it becomes available and upload again.

Some optional fields were flagged as missing

This is not a failed upload. House Accounts analyzes whatever information is available and simply lets you know when a helpful column (like contact email or margin) was not found, so you know what to add later if you want stronger recommendations.

My software isn't listed here

Use the Generic Excel guide -- export whatever report your system offers and upload it as CSV. House Accounts works with most standard exports without modification.
