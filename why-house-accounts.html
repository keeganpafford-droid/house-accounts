Why House Accounts

Who should I contact next, and why?

That question is the whole product. Here's where it came from.

Built from the work, not from theory.

House Accounts started with a simple observation from actually selling promotional products, not a theory about what sales teams need. Its founder entered the industry with no prior experience and grew a book of business from roughly $0 to $1M. A number of things contributed to that growth — but a specific set of habits, applied consistently to the customers already on the books, kept showing up as useful:

  • Catching reorders before a customer had to ask.
  • Noticing when an account was starting to go quiet.
  • Finding a timely, real reason to reach out.
  • Understanding what was actually happening inside a customer's business.
  • Expanding relationships across departments and categories.
  • Being proactive, instead of waiting for the customer to bring it up.

One rep can do this. A team can't — not consistently.

Those habits work. But doing them by hand, across an entire book of accounts, takes constant attention — and making them consistent across an entire sales team is even harder. What one strong rep does instinctively is genuinely difficult to teach, remember, and repeat at scale.

What we built

House Accounts systematizes that attention. It watches the customers you already have, and surfaces the same kind of timely reasons to reach out that used to depend on one rep's memory — so every rep on the team can work the way the best rep already does.

Make every rep their best rep.
